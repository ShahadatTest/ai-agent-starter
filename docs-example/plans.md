# Plans and pricing

Northwind Notes has three plans. Prices are in US dollars and billed in advance.

## Free

Up to 3 notebooks and 100 MB of attachments. Sync on two devices. No credit card required.

## Pro

$6 per month, or $60 per year (two months free). Unlimited notebooks, 10 GB of attachments, sync on unlimited
devices, version history for 30 days and offline access on mobile.

## Team

$12 per user per month, billed annually only. Everything in Pro, plus shared notebooks, admin console,
single sign-on (SAML) and version history for 1 year. Minimum of 3 users.

## Discounts

Students and teachers get 50% off Pro with a school email address. Non-profits get 30% off Team.
Discounts do not stack.
