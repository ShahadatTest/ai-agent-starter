# Refunds and cancellation

## Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from Settings → Billing. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you
already paid for, then the account moves to the Free plan. Notes are never deleted when you downgrade; notebooks
above the Free limit become read-only.

## Refund window

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase or renewal. After 14 days, annual plans are not
refunded, but you keep access until the end of the year.

Monthly plans are not refunded, because you can cancel before the next charge.

## How to ask for a refund

Write to billing@northwind-notes.example with the email of your account and the date of the charge. Refunds go back
to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.
