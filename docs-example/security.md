# Security and privacy

## Encryption

Notes are encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Pro and Team users can turn on end-to-end
encryption for individual notebooks; we cannot read or recover those notebooks if you lose your passphrase.

## Two-factor authentication

All plans support two-factor authentication with an authenticator app or a hardware security key (FIDO2).
Team admins can require it for every member.

## Data location

Data is stored in the European Union (Frankfurt) by default. Team customers can choose the United States instead
when the workspace is created; the region cannot be changed later.

## Exporting your data

You can export everything as Markdown files at any time from Settings → Export, on every plan, including Free.
